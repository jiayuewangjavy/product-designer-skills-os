# Product Design Loop Contract

Every skill may be called independently. A composed loop keeps one collaboration and decision spine plus a five-part learning readout.

```markdown
## Collaboration & decision spine
- Working artifact / source of truth:
- Current working surface:
- Decision owner:
- Contributors and agents:
- Human / AI authority boundary:
- Required review or approval:

## What we knew
- Evidence:
- Inference:
- Assumption:
- Confirmed decision:
- Preference:

## Decision now

## What we made
- Artifact:
- Question it was intended to answer:

## What we learned
- New evidence:
- Confidence and limitations:

## What changed
- Context update:
- Decision changed, retained, or rejected:
- Reason:

## Next unknown
- Highest-risk unknown:
- Smallest next evidence:
```

The spine may stay stable while work moves from source evidence to canvas, prototype, code, or a live product. Update it whenever ownership, authority, or the canonical artifact changes.

An artifact does not close a loop. A loop closes only when evidence is interpreted and a product decision is explicitly retained, changed, rejected, or deferred by the accountable owner.
