# Product Design Loop Contract

Every skill may be called independently, but a composed loop must preserve five fields:

```markdown
## What we knew
- Evidence:
- Inference:
- Assumption:
- Confirmed decision:
- Preference:

## Decision now

## What we made
- Artifact:
- Question it was intended to answer:

## What we learned
- New evidence:
- Confidence and limitations:

## What changed
- Context update:
- Decision changed, retained, or rejected:
- Reason:

## Next unknown
- Highest-risk unknown:
- Smallest next evidence:
```

An artifact does not close a loop. A loop closes only when evidence is interpreted and a product decision is explicitly retained, changed, rejected, or deferred.
