# Contributing to Product Designer Skills OS

Thank you for helping improve the uncertainty-driven product design skill network.

## Before contributing

- Search [existing issues](https://github.com/jiayuewangjavy/product-designer-skills-os/issues) before proposing a new skill or major behavior change.
- Describe the product decision, uncertainty, evidence, and expected learning value behind a proposed change.
- For security-sensitive reports or private information, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md); never include credentials, customer data, or confidential employer materials in public issues.

## Pull requests

1. Create a focused branch and explain the behavior or documentation change.
2. Keep the Router's decision ownership and human-approval boundaries intact.
3. Update examples or evaluation notes when behavior changes.
4. Run `python scripts/validate_skills.py` and report the result.
5. Identify third-party sources and their licenses; do not paste unlicensed or incompatible implementations.
6. Avoid committing secrets, proprietary company assets, or material you are not authorized to contribute.

## Attribution and provenance

Document material influences, reviewed versions, and license boundaries in [references/SOURCES.md](./references/SOURCES.md) when relevant. Keep authorship and copyright notices accurate.

## Community conduct

Be constructive, specific, and respectful. Focus critique on observable behavior and evidence rather than personal judgments.
